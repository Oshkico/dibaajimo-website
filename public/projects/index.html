Projects

Join astronaut Marshmallow and their co-pilot, Berry the engineer, on a thrilling space adventure! When their ship collides with a meteorite, they crash-land on a lush, mysterious planet. Stranded, they must explore this new world to find the resources needed to repair their ship. Along the way, they encounter the Novarians, an Anishinaabe mushroom-based species. With their new friends, Marsh, Berry, and Nova embark on an exciting journey, meeting unique characters and discovering the Anishinaabe mythology spread across Turtle Island. Planet Nova will have 8, 11 minute episodes. Planet Nova will be offered in English and in Anishinaabemowin. Lip syncing will be animated in both English and Anishinaabemowin.
